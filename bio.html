Awards

Presidential Award, Republic of Korea · 2019

For the world's first 32TB dual-port SAS enterprise SSD, the Samsung PM1643. I cut the blocks reserved for metadata by estimating lifetime program and erase cycles against a five-year, three-drive-writes-per-day target, which returned those blocks to user capacity and raised throughput by 24 percent, and split metadata work into atomic units that cut metadata-induced latency by 30 percent.

CES Innovation Award · 2022

For the world's first PCIe Gen5 enterprise SSD, the Samsung PM1743. Keeping the whole metadata cache in DRAM forces a DRAM budget near one thousandth of device capacity, which caps how large a drive can get. I built a partial DRAM scheme that uses NAND flash as the final cache stage, segmented for hit rate, with read and write caches split so write latency hides entirely, and a dirty-cache program scheme that writes back only dirty entries. It let drives ship past 32TB on less DRAM.

Samsung Best Paper Award · 2022

First author on "STM: Improving the Reliability of SSD through Selective Trim Manager". Metadata blocks wear out under repeated shutdowns and large trims. The paper proposes a dynamic shutdown manager that skips the shutdown write when open-time requirements can be met without it, and a selective trim manager that ignores trims for logical zones already empty. Both shipped, and cut metadata wear-out by more than 25 percent under normal workloads.
