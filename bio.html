I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Stanford, advised by Philip Levis, working on memory systems. I came to research after nine years building enterprise SSD firmware at Samsung.

Education

Stanford University

PhD, Electrical Engineering, expected 2029. Advised by Philip Levis in the Stanford Information Networks Group. My work is on memory beyond DRAM and the operating system support it needs.

Industry

Samsung Electronics, 2016 to 2024

Nine years on enterprise SSD firmware, latterly as a senior software engineer and associate architect. My work covered metadata management and power-loss protection for datacenter drives, including a PCIe Gen5 enterprise SSD and a 32TB SAS SSD.

Awards

2022 · Samsung paper award, for work on SSD reliability.
2022 · CES Innovation Award, for a PCIe Gen5 enterprise SSD.
2019 · Samsung President's Award, for a 32TB SAS SSD.
